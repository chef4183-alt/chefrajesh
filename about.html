A Refined Culinary Journey

♨

Chef Rajesh Abraham began his culinary journey in 2000 and has built a career spanning over two decades across the UAE, Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, and India.

With strong expertise in menu development, kitchen operations, and team leadership, he combines culinary creativity with operational discipline to deliver refined and memorable dining experiences.

His philosophy is centred on using the right ingredients, technique, temperature, and timing to create balanced and satisfying cuisine that nourishes both body and soul.
