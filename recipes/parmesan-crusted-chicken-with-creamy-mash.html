Parmesan Crusted Chicken with Creamy Mash

By Chef Rajesh Abraham

Prep:10 minutes
Cook:20 minutes
Serves:2
Total:30 minutes
← Back to All Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 1/4 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 2 large potatoes
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup milk or cream
  • Extra parmesan for garnish

The Method

How to Make It

Tender chicken breast coated in a savory parmesan crust, pan-seared until golden and served with smooth creamy mashed potatoes. This comforting dish balances rich flavor with a crisp texture, making it a simple yet elegant meal.

PARMESAN CRUSTED CHICKEN WITH CREAMY MASH_01.webp Hero / preparation photo
  1. 01

    Season chicken breasts with salt and pepper.

    Coat chicken with a mixture of parmesan cheese and breadcrumbs. Heat olive oil in a pan and cook chicken until golden and fully cooked.

  2. 02

    Garnish with grated parmesan and serve warm.
