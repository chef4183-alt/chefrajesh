Hulk's Green Smoothie Bowl

By Chef Rajesh Abraham

Prep:5 minutes
Cook:0 minutes
Serves:1–2
Total:5 minutes
← Back to All Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup fresh pineapple chunks
  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1 cup fresh spinach
  • ½ cup yogurt or plant-based yogurt
  • ½ cup chilled coconut water or milk
  • 1 tsp honey (optional)
  • 1 tbsp sliced almonds
  • 1 tbsp raisins

The Method

How to Make It

Hulk's Green Power Smoothie Bowl A vibrant, nutrient-packed smoothie bowl inspired by the strength and color of the Hulk. Blended with pineapple, banana, spinach, and fresh herbs, this refreshing bowl delivers natural energy, antioxidants, and healthy fiber. Topped with almonds and raisins for extra texture and nutrition.

HULK'S GREEN SMOOTHIE BOWL_01.webp Hero / preparation photo
  1. 01

    Add pineapple, banana, spinach, yogurt, and coconut water into a blender.

  2. 02

    Pour into a bowl.

    Top with sliced almonds and raisins for texture and natural sweetness.
