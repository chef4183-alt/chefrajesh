Herb-Grilled Beef Steak with Thyme Roasted Potatoes

By Chef Rajesh Abraham

🕐Prep:20 min
🔥Cook:30 min
👥Serves:2
⭐Skill:Intermediate
← Back to All Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 beef sirloin or ribeye steaks (200–220g each)
  • 4–5 sprigs fresh thyme
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • to taste salt & black pepper
  • 400g baby potatoes, halved
  • 1 tbsp rosemary, chopped
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 30g butter

Categories

Anytime Worth the Wait

Chef's Note

"The secret to a perfect steak lies in resting it properly. Never skip this step — it allows the juices to redistribute and creates that melt-in-the-mouth texture." — Chef Rajesh Abraham

The Method

How to Make It

A classic steak preparation elevated with fresh herbs and a Dijon mustard crust, paired with perfectly crispy thyme-roasted baby potatoes. Quality ingredients, the right technique, and a little patience produce extraordinary results.

01

Marinate the Steak

Pat the steaks dry with kitchen paper. Mix olive oil, minced garlic, Dijon mustard, fresh thyme leaves, salt, and black pepper into a paste. Coat the steaks generously and let marinate for at least 30 minutes at room temperature — or overnight in the refrigerator for deeper flavour.

02

Prepare the Potatoes

Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Toss the halved baby potatoes with olive oil, chopped rosemary, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Spread on a baking tray in a single layer and roast for 25–30 minutes, turning once, until golden and crispy.

Potatoes in roasting tray ★ Add a photo here · 700×395px
03

Grill the Steak

Heat a cast-iron griddle or heavy skillet over high heat until smoking. Add a little oil. Place steaks on the grill — do not move them. Cook 3–4 minutes per side for medium-rare. In the last minute, add butter and a thyme sprig and baste continuously.

04

Rest the Steak

Remove from heat and rest on a warm plate, loosely covered with foil, for 5–7 minutes. Resting allows the muscle fibres to relax and the juices to redistribute throughout the meat — never skip this step.

05

Plate & Serve

Slice against the grain for maximum tenderness. Arrange alongside the thyme-roasted potatoes. Drizzle resting juices over the steak. Finish with a fresh thyme sprig and a pinch of flaked sea salt.

♨ Chef Rajesh's Tips

  • Always bring steak to room temperature before cooking — cold meat will steam instead of sear.
  • Use a meat thermometer: 52°C for rare, 57°C for medium-rare, 63°C for medium.
  • Never press down on the steak — you'll lose precious juices.
  • The colour of the flesh is your best guide — practice makes perfect.
  • Leftover potatoes? Reheat in a hot oven for 8 minutes to restore crispiness.
